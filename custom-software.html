All-Industry Custom Billing Software

Bespoke POS & invoicing engines for retail shops, supermarkets, wholesale distributors, pharmacies, clinics, salons, and auto workshops. Customized barcode formats, GST/VAT compliance, discount rules, and thermal/A4 invoices.

  • Multi-Format Output: Seamless printing on 2-inch & 3-inch ESC/POS thermal printers, full-size A4/A5 laser invoices, and instant digital WhatsApp PDF receipts.
  • Bespoke Tax & GST Engine: Multi-tier slab calculations, automated HSN/SAC codes, reverse charge mechanism (RCM), and integrated E-Invoicing / E-Way bill generation.
