CORE INFRASTRUCTURE

Built for Operators Who Value Precision and Zero Downtime.

Modern hospitality demands instantaneous transaction processing and airtight audit controls.

01

Sub-Second Billing & KOT Dispatch

Execute split bills, combo modifiers, dynamic table transfers, and kitchen order tickets in under 3 taps with keyboard hotkeys or touchscreen gestures.

02

Local-First Offline Resiliency

Internet outages will never stop your service. All local terminals communicate across the local LAN and automatically sync to the cloud once connectivity resumes.

03

Live Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Color-coded prep timers, station routing (grill, bar, fry, pantry), item aggregation, and bump bar support eliminate printed paper tickets entirely.

04

Real-Time Recipe & Stock Tracking

Automatic inventory deduction per sold menu item down to grams and milliliters. Receive low-stock alerts and auto-generate replenishment purchase orders.

05

Hotel Property Management (PMS)

Manage room availability calendars, guest check-in/check-out, night audit closures, room service billing posted to room folios, and housekeeping states.

06

Multi-Branch Central Control

Push universal menu updates, regional catalog configurations, staff permissions, and central warehouse commissary dispatches to 50+ locations in a single click.
