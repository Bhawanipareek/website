Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear, authoritative answers about BillingServer implementation, pricing, hardware, and multi-unit support.

BillingServer is a unified enterprise hospitality management platform designed specifically for restaurants, hotels, cafes, and multi-branch hospitality groups. It integrates high-speed point-of-sale billing, table management, Kitchen Display Systems (KDS), hotel room folios, central inventory, and real-time business reporting in one centralized software suite.

BillingServer is designed for businesses of all scales in the food service and lodging industries: fine dining restaurants, casual eateries, boutique hotels and resorts, quick-service counters (QSR), cafes, bakeries, bars, and multi-outlet food court chains requiring centralized brand oversight.

Yes. BillingServer natively unifies Hotel Property Management (PMS) and Restaurant Point of Sale (POS) under a single database architecture. Dining charges, bar tabs, and room-service orders can be posted directly to an in-house guest's room folio with one click, completely eliminating manual billing reconciliation between departments.

Yes. BillingServer features an enterprise Multi-Branch HQ portal. You can manage multiple restaurant locations, hotel branches, or franchise outlets from a single master dashboard. Features include global master menu publishing, cross-branch inventory transfers, centralized supplier management, and consolidated executive sales reports.

Yes. BillingServer offers a native, high-performance Windows desktop application optimized for counter cashiers and touchscreen POS terminals. It includes full keyboard shortcut support (F1–F12 quick billing), direct ESC/POS thermal printing, and an offline-first sync engine that guarantees billing continues uninterrupted even during internet downtime.

Yes. BillingServer includes contact-free dynamic QR code ordering. Guests can scan table QR codes or in-room QR stands on their smartphones to browse digital interactive menus, submit orders straight to the kitchen KDS, and request their final bill without waiting for waitstaff.

Yes. BillingServer provides native support for all standard 2-inch and 3-inch ESC/POS thermal receipt and kitchen ticket printers. Supported connection interfaces include USB, Ethernet (LAN), Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth across industry-standard hardware brands including Epson, Star Micronics, Citizen, and Sunmi.
